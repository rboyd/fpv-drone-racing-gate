Review / assembly

Select scenes 01–12 in Blender's scene selector. Objects and collections remain editable; the guide is also embedded in Blender's text blocks. The face docks detach from PVC before folding. The frame splits at its four midpoints, and travels separately.

Model checks: exact ring coverage, non-overlapping stock cuts, uniform inserts, transport leaf lengths, coupon manifoldness and print-bed envelopes. See layout validation and coupon geometry.
